How the Hybrid LSB-DCT Engine Works

        
A balanced fusion of frequency domain resilience and spatial domain imperceptibility.

    


    

        

            
1

            
Color Conversion (YCbCr)

            
The RGB image is transformed into YCbCr color space. Embedding is performed on the Luminance (Y) channel, which human vision is less sensitive to high-frequency variations in.

        

        

            
2

            
8x8 Block 2D-DCT

            
The Y channel is divided into non-overlapping 8x8 blocks. 2D Discrete Cosine Transform converts each spatial block into frequency coefficients (DC and AC components).

        

        

            
3

            
Quantized LSB Embedding

            
The mid-frequency coefficient at index (4, 3) is quantized with factor \(Q=20\). The secret bit is embedded into its Least Significant Bit (LSB), preventing compression distortion.

        

        

            
4

            
Inverse DCT & Metric Check

            
2D Inverse DCT reconstructs the spatial pixels. Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) is evaluated, maintaining pristine visual fidelity (\(> 40\,\text{dB}\)).
